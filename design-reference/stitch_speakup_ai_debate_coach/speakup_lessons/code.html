LOGIC

Foundations of Argument

schedule 15 min read
star Difficulty: Beginner

In the realm of debate, an argument is not a quarrel. It is a series of statements—the premises—intended to determine the degree of truth of another statement—the conclusion.

The cornerstone of this process is the Syllogism. A syllogism is a kind of logical argument that applies deductive reasoning to arrive at a conclusion based on two or more propositions that are asserted or assumed to be true.

AI TIP

"Focus on clarity first. A complex argument built on a shaky premise will always fail under scrutiny."
